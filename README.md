# 🏥 Healthcare Claim Denial Analysis

## Project Overview

This is an end-to-end healthcare business analytics project that investigates outpatient claim denials and identifies operational opportunities to reduce preventable revenue loss.

The project combines Python-based data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business analytics, and data visualization to transform raw operational data into actionable business insights.

The final deliverable includes an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to support operational monitoring and decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Claim denials are one of the most common challenges in healthcare revenue cycle management. They delay reimbursement, increase administrative workload, and negatively impact organizational financial performance.

Many denials originate from preventable front-end registration issues, including incorrect primary insurance selection, inactive insurance coverage, and incomplete eligibility verification.

The goal of this project is to identify the operational drivers behind outpatient claim denials and provide data-driven recommendations that can improve registration quality and reduce preventable denials.

---

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Audit and clean operational healthcare data.
- Explore denial patterns across departments and payer groups.
- Identify the most common front-end registration issues.
- Evaluate the financial impact of different denial reasons.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for operational monitoring.

---

## Dataset

This project uses a simulated outpatient claim dataset representing patient encounters across multiple clinical departments.

The dataset includes:

- Patient encounters
- Clinical departments
- Visit types
- Insurance payer groups
- Coverage status
- Front-end registration issues
- Claim denial status
- Denial reasons
- Financial impact

The simulated dataset was designed to reflect realistic healthcare revenue cycle workflows while protecting patient privacy.
